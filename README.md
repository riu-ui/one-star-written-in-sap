A tiny program for the make.fun bounty "One Star, Written in Sap". It writes one original one-line 1-star review, in the voice of a houseplant reviewing its human owner, to `entry.txt`. The joke is a built one, not just a cute idea: the owner keeps searching for why his plant is dying while holding the empty watering can — the answer to his mystery is literally in his hand.

The entry line (136 characters):

> One star: he keeps googling 'why is my plant dying' while holding a bone-dry watering can, as if the answer isn't literally in his hand.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (or `$OUTPUT_DIR`, or `/output` by default) — one line, no trailing newline, deterministic on every run.
